Gameplay

The app offers one classic 32-card Deberts preset, local computer opponents, Game Center multiplayer and a manual scorekeeper. Regional conventions vary. The scorekeeper lets your table choose deal outcomes; additional house-rule penalties are entered manually. No real-money betting, prizes, purchases or ads are offered.

Online availability

Online games depend on Apple's Game Center, compatible app versions and participants' connections. The host holds the complete match. Closing the host app ends the room; host migration and persistent online saves are not supported.

Local data

A new local game replaces the previous local table save. Physical-card score sheets are stored separately. Removing the app removes local data, subject to your backup settings.
