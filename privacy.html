Deberts — Privacy Policy

Updated 14 October 2026. Developer: DmytroGusev on GitHub.

Local games and score sheets

Names entered for physical-card games, deal history and saved local games are stored on your device. We do not upload these saves to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Removing the app removes local app data, subject to backup settings.

Game Center

Online play uses Apple's Game Center and GameKit services. Your Game Center display name, game-specific player identifier and necessary gameplay state are exchanged with match participants through Apple services. Each player receives their own hand and the public table state. The host device holds the complete match. We do not operate an online game server or store your Game Center credentials.

Apple handles Game Center authentication and service data under Apple's privacy policy. Refer to your device's Game Center settings for your Apple account and service preferences.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app includes no advertising, developer analytics SDKs, tracking, in-app purchases or real-money betting. We do not collect gameplay or score-sheet data on our own servers. Apple may process diagnostic data according to your device and App Store settings.

Support

If you choose to post a GitHub issue, information you submit is public and processed by GitHub under its own policies. Avoid private account details. For support or privacy questions, use the support issue tracker.
